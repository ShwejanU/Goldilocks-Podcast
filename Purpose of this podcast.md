# Goldilocks Podcast *Origin of the name* 
The name comes from the famous story of Goldilocks and the three bears. Goldilocks tries each bears items and life’s choices but there was no definitive choice that one was right. She picked and chose the option that was right for her. We think life is similar. No one’s path is going to be right for you but by giving you a plethora of paths to hear from Maybe one might resonate with you. And so you take a piece of our podcast with you for the rest of your life. 

# Founder philosophy:
Taking the long term bet on people. A person’s intent is the directionality of their lives. There will be ups and downs but as long as someone has the right intent it is usually worth taking a bet on them. 

***How do we tie that to the podcast?*** 
We don’t mind bringing on people that aren’t famous and don’t have traction just yet to hear their stories.

# Interview Style 

Try to let conversation flow naturally, and lean into interviewer's passions, but be prepared with a note sheet with questions based on a study of the person's life story beforehand.  Group questions based on life stage so that you come prepared for any situation and you **have a good understand of the interviewee before the podcast starts.**
