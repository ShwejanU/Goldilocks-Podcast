Historical context
**Serendipity** is the occurrence of **valuable or beneficial discoveries that happen unexpectedly while pursuing something else**. A low cost, high impact way to generate serendipity is to have people of different backgrounds connect socially spontaneously and birth ideas together.

Many past innovations were born out of serendipitous connection between people of different disciples bringing their fields together and creating something new. For example in renaissance Italy bankers, scientists and artists would inhabit the same social spaces, giving birth to the renaissance architectural style and numerous important scientists, for example Leonardo Da Vinci. Bell Labs, which helped commercialize many important technologies key to the digital era such as C and C++ programming languages, the transistor and the fiber optic cable, was an incredibly serendipitous environment bringing together top physicists, financiers, mathematicians and engineers spurring unexpected innovation. 

Levels of spontaneous social connection have been dropping significantly according to statistics- prominent psychologists and social scientists, such as Jonathan Haidt in his book "The Anxious Generation" and Robert Putnam in "Bowling Alone" attribute cause to screen-based entertainment.

What is the future of American Innovation in culture and technology in context of a rapid decline in serendipitous connection due to the proliferation of screen-based entertainment?

Story 
Gave up using a smartphone when he was 16 and switched to a cell phone due to him believing that smartphones were affecting his mental health negatively. As Covid-19 rolled around (I believe he gave up his phone before covid) spaces where people serendipitously hang out with their full, undivided presence shut down en masse. Mental health concerns and loneliness skyrocketed, and the issues Sean cared about became a global problem.

Started Reconnect as a student club in order to create spontaneous human connection in the fashion of previous eras. Started multiple clubs in florida, and then he got significant press coverage being covered by 6 major media outlets. He dropped out of school to pursue reconnect full time, and has now been working on learning how to fundraise for the organization.
