## Coverage gaps

This is as complete as public sources allow, but it is not certifiably exhaustive:

1. **Visiting Group Partner cohorts for W23, S25, W25, W26 and S26 have no dedicated YC blog announcement** that I could find. Some names in those batches are likely missing.
2. **Archive.org is inaccessible to me**, so I could not diff historical snapshots of `ycombinator.com/people` — which is the only place short-tenure partners who never got an announcement post would appear.
3. **End dates are often unannounced.** YC publicizes arrivals far more consistently than departures; most `~` dates are inferred from when a person stopped appearing on the People page or from their own posts.
4. **Role boundaries are fuzzy.** YC has used Partner, Group Partner, General Partner, Managing Partner, Visiting Group Partner, part-time Partner, Partner Emeritus, and program-specific partners (Continuity, YC China, Startup School). Some non-investing staff carry partner-level titles (Legal, Finance, Partnerships) and are separated out above.
